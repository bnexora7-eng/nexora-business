## Positioning

NEXORA Payment Integrity is an early-stage, pre-production prototype exploring explainable screening of synthetic payment-event notifications. It demonstrates deterministic ACCEPT, REVIEW and REJECT outcomes and duplicate-event awareness.

The campaign objective is not to sell a finished payment product. It is to secure technical feedback, customer-discovery conversations and a potential design partner for a tightly scoped prototype or pilot.

## Main advertisement — English

### Make payment events clearer.

Payment workflows depend on reliable event handling. But when events are repeated, delayed or need attention, teams need clear outcomes and traceable reasoning.

**NEXORA Payment Integrity** is a focused prototype exploring a simple screening workflow for payment-event notifications:

- **ACCEPT** — the synthetic event passes the current demo rules.
- **REVIEW** — the event needs an operator's attention.
- **REJECT** — the event fails a demo rule, including duplicate-event checks.

Explore the interactive demonstration and see how explainable outcomes could fit into a payment operations workflow.

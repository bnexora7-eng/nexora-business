# ALPINOSS pricing and brand references

## Central pricing page

The single public pricing destination is `/pricing/`, linked from the main navigation menu as **Prices & Payments**.

Published indicative one-time service offers:
- Business analysis — €149
- AI assistant prototype — €295
- Business-specific AI solution — €495
- System integration — from €950
- Multi-step automation — €950–€2,500
- Enterprise projects — €5,000–€15,000+

ALPINOSS uses one-time project/service engagements. Do not advertise a monthly membership or recurring platform fee.

## Individually agreed project pricing and payment terms

The public price list contains indicative prices for defined service types and business sizes. The final project price is discussed and agreed individually after the scope, deliverables and timeline are understood.

Payment method, schedule and any milestones are also agreed individually and confirmed in the written quotation. There is no default 50/50 split and no automatic payment commitment on the pricing page. The pricing page is informational and does not itself collect payment.

## Brand artwork status

The SVG files currently in the repository are provisional vector concepts, not verified copies of the exact original logo/profile artwork. The CSS background is a visual reference approximation, not a backup of the original background image.

The exact original artwork must be supplied as image files before it can be archived faithfully. See `brand/README.md` for the preservation procedure. Do not represent the provisional SVGs as the owner's final approved logo.

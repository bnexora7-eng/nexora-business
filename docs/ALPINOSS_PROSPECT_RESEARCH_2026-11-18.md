Internal research only. No prospects have been contacted. Public signals are evidence of process context, not proof that a company wants to buy from ALPINOSS.

## Commercial hypothesis

Offer a tightly scoped workflow-improvement pilot: map one repetitive finance/operations process, identify exceptions and handoffs, then demonstrate a controlled workflow with validation, traceability and human approval. Do not pitch a generic invoice OCR replacement or claim production integration before access, API capability, security and acceptance criteria are confirmed.

## Market caution

Accounting platforms already offer invoice recognition, AI agents, bank matching and workflow automation. A generic “AI invoice scanner” is not differentiated. ALPINOSS should sell a measurable gap-filler around exceptions, cross-system handoffs, audit evidence and approval controls — only after confirming that the prospect's current stack does not already solve it.

## Proposed first paid engagement

**Workflow Control Assessment + Demonstrator**
- One process, one department, synthetic or anonymised sample data only.
- Deliverables: process map, exception taxonomy, clickable/demo workflow, validation rules, audit/approval design, integration feasibility note and fixed implementation estimate.
- Indicative starting price to test: €500–€950 for a tightly bounded assessment and demonstrator. This is ALPINOSS's proposed price, not a market-verified rate or an offer accepted by any prospect.
- Production integration, hosting, security review, data-processing agreement and ongoing support are separate scopes.

## Next qualification questions (before quoting implementation)
1. Which exact task still requires manual copying, checking or chasing?
2. How many cases/documents per week and how much staff time does it consume?
3. Which systems are involved (Exact Online, AFAS, Excel, SharePoint, email, project software)?
4. What exceptions cause rework, delays or audit risk?
5. Can they provide synthetic/anonymised sample data and a process owner for a short online discovery?

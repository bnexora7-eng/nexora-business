## Full description draft

NEXORA brings business planning, community updates and marketplace discovery together in one mobile workspace.

With NEXORA you can:
- Create an account and manage your profile.
- Prepare business action plans, SWOT notes and execution checklists.
- Save your business workspace to your account.
- Share updates with the NEXORA Community.
- Browse and publish marketplace offers.
- Explore a payment-event screening demonstration with illustrative ACCEPT, REVIEW and REJECT outcomes.
- Delete your account and associated content from the app.

**Important product information**

NEXORA is an early-stage prototype. Some online features depend on the availability and configuration of the NEXORA server. Payment Integrity is a deterministic demonstration using sample inputs; it is not certified fraud detection and does not connect to banks. Marketplace checkout, seller payouts, paid subscriptions, cash rewards and bank connections are not enabled. Mollie checkout and AI-generated business planning may remain unavailable until their respective service configuration and testing are complete. Do not submit sensitive personal, banking or payment information.

## 2. Free account

Creating a ALPINOSS account does not require a subscription or payment. Account access is separate from any paid custom project engagement. Account registration, email verification, privacy, acceptable-use and deletion terms must be completed and reviewed before launch.

## 3. Project enquiry, scope and quotation

A website enquiry is non-binding and is not an order. ALPINOSS must assess feasibility, requirements, deliverables, dependencies, exclusions, delivery timeline, support/maintenance and price before offering a project.

Each paid engagement requires a written quotation or statement of work identifying:
- the contracting parties and the legal supplier;
- the specific service and measurable deliverables;
- customer responsibilities, required access and third-party dependencies;
- total fee, applicable VAT and currency;
- payment milestones and due dates;
- delivery/acceptance criteria and change-control process;
- cancellation, refund, delay, warranty/conformity and support arrangements;
- intellectual-property ownership and permitted use;
- data-processing/security responsibilities where customer data is involved.

No work or payment commitment should be inferred from an enquiry or indicative price list alone.

## 5. Invoicing and payment

Payment checkout is disabled by default. No provider is approved for live ALPINOSS transactions at this stage. PayPal is excluded.

Before requesting payment:
- confirm the contracting legal entity/operator and its registration/tax position;
- confirm a suitable business bank account and payment-provider approval;
- determine the VAT treatment for the customer's location and B2B/B2C status;
- issue a compliant invoice or payment request with required supplier/customer details, sequential invoice number, dates, clear service description, taxable amount, VAT rate and VAT amount or the correct exemption/reverse-charge wording;
- state payment due date, refund/cancellation rules and support contact.

A browser redirect is not proof of payment. Any future checkout must reconcile the exact order, amount, currency and provider status server-side and safely handle duplicate webhooks, failed/canceled/refunded payments and disputes.

### Project payment milestones — required in every quotation

Each project is paid as a **one-time project fee**, which may be split into an advance payment and a final balance. The split is agreed individually for each project; it is not automatically 50/50 and no fixed percentage is promised. The written quotation must state the exact advance amount or percentage, the amount remaining, and the due date or objective milestone for each instalment. As a working commercial option, the advance may be 25% with 75% due at completion, but the actual split must reflect project size, duration, risk and agreed deliverables.

Unless the signed quotation expressly states otherwise and applicable law permits it, work starts only after the agreed advance has cleared. The remaining balance becomes due after the deliverables have been completed and presented for the acceptance process defined in the quotation. For longer projects, the parties may agree additional milestone payments in writing. Any cancellation, refund, withdrawal or dispute is handled under the contract and mandatory statutory rights; an advance is not automatically non-refundable.

## 6. Consumer rights and cancellation

Some customers may have statutory distance-contract withdrawal rights, digital-service conformity rights or other mandatory consumer protections. The exact treatment depends on the customer, service, contract and applicable Dutch/EU law. Do not use a blanket “no refunds” term or require a customer to waive statutory rights without a legally valid basis.

A Dutch/EU legal professional must review withdrawal instructions, early-start requests, proportional charges, cancellation, refunds, statutory remedies and any exceptions before the terms are published.

## 7. Privacy, security and customer data

Do not request bank credentials, card data or unnecessary sensitive personal data. Customer data access, processing roles, retention, deletion, subprocessors, international transfers, security measures and any data-processing agreement must be defined before a customer project uses personal data.

## 8. Intellectual property and third-party services

The quotation must identify pre-existing ALPINOSS materials, customer materials, newly created deliverables, licence/transfer terms, open-source components and third-party provider costs. Ownership must not be assumed or transferred by silence.

NEXORA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how NEXORA BUSINESS handles personal data in the NEXORA mobile application and related services. NEXORA is currently a pre-production prototype. Do not submit sensitive personal, banking, payment-card or health information.

1. Who is responsible

For privacy questions and requests, contact the NEXORA project operator at bnexora7@gmail.com. Please include “Privacy request” in the subject. NEXORA is currently a project name. The responsible operator's full legal identity and postal contact address must be published here before public production launch.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: email address, a salted password hash, account identifier, server-side session-token hash, and account/session timestamps. When you sign in through the website, a temporary bearer token is held in your browser's session storage for that tab/session.
  • Community data: posts you choose to publish. Community posts are visible to other users.
  • Marketplace data: offer title, description, category, price and publication time. Published offers are visible to other users.
  • Business workspace: business name, tagline and notes/goals saved to your account. These are intended to be private to your account.
  • Payment records: if checkout is enabled, order amount, currency, description, provider reference and payment status. Card and bank login credentials are not collected by NEXORA; the payment provider handles checkout.
  • AI planning inputs: if server-side AI planning is enabled and you use it, the business details you submit are sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response.
  • Technical/security data: limited request and diagnostic information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is used to create and secure accounts, provide requested features, store content, process and reconcile checkout orders when enabled, respond to support/privacy requests, prevent abuse and maintain the service. Depending on the feature and applicable law, processing is based on performance of a requested service, legitimate interests in service security and operation, consent where required, or legal obligations.

4. Service providers and sharing

Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the API and database (currently configured for Render), by FormSubmit when you voluntarily send a project enquiry through the website form, by Mollie if payment checkout is enabled, and by the configured AI provider if you request AI-generated business planning. FormSubmit receives the enquiry fields and forwards the submission to the NEXORA contact mailbox; its documentation states that submissions are retained for 30 days. Review the provider's privacy information before submitting. These providers process data to provide their services under their own terms and privacy documentation. NEXORA does not sell personal data and does not use the app for advertising.

5. Public and private content

Community posts and Marketplace offers are designed to be public to other app users. Do not include confidential or sensitive information in them. Business workspace notes are associated with your account and are not intended to be public.

6. Retention

Account data and user-generated content are retained while your account is active. When an account is deleted, its account record, sessions, community posts, marketplace offers and business workspace are deleted. A limited payment ledger record may be retained without its account link where required for accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Technical logs are retained only as needed for security, troubleshooting and legal requirements.

7. Account deletion and your rights

You can delete your account from Profile → Delete account and data in the app. You can also use the web account-deletion page. The request requires your account email and password. Deletion removes the account and associated content; legally required transaction records may be retained in an unlinked form. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, object to or request portability/deletion of personal data. Contact bnexora7@gmail.com.

8. Your rights and complaints

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact bnexora7@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

9. International transfers

Our infrastructure is configured for hosting in the EU region where supported. Some service providers, including AI services if enabled, may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, such transfers must rely on an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. The exact provider configuration must be confirmed before production launch.

10. Security

Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, session tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes, and app sessions are transmitted using HTTPS when the service is correctly deployed. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected security issues to the contact email above.

11. Children

NEXORA is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly seek to collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted.

12. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the product changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Contact bnexora7@gmail.com with questions.

Prototype notice: NEXORA's payment and AI features may be unavailable or limited until their respective provider accounts and production safeguards are configured. Do not treat the prototype as a banking or regulated payment service.

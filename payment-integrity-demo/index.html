What we are building

Make payment events easier to understand and control.

Payment operations can involve repeated events, inconsistent identifiers and unclear exceptions. NEXORA focuses on turning normalized event information into structured, explainable screening outcomes.

⧉ Duplicate event detection

Identify repeated provider and event identifiers within a configured screening workflow.

⌕ Review signals

Highlight events that meet configured review conditions, with a reason attached to the result.

✓ Clear decisions

Present structured ACCEPT, REVIEW or REJECT outcomes for operational review.

A practical workflow

From event data to a clearer next step.

The current demonstrator uses simulated payment-event data and deterministic prototype rules. The workflow below describes the intended service direction.

01 / SUBMIT

Normalize the event

Provide agreed event fields through a defined integration or controlled test workflow.

02 / SCREEN

Apply screening rules

Check identifiers and configured conditions to produce a structured result.

03 / REVIEW

Understand the outcome

Use the decision and its explanation to support an operational review process.
